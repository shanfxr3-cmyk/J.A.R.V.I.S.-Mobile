Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Short version: don't be creepy, don't be shady, don't pass my work off as yours.

## Please don't
- Use the camera, microphone or wake word to spy on, stalk, harass or record people without their consent.
- Use Jarvis to make or spread illegal content, scams, malware, threats, or content that exploits or harms children.
- Use it to impersonate someone or to deceive people in a harmful way.
- Hammer the AI or voice services with automated abuse, or try to dodge their limits.
- Share your API keys in public, or use somebody else's keys without permission.
- Re-upload my official APK under your own name, or strip the credit from a copy.
- Use the "Hey Jarvis" wake-word model in a commercial product. It is non-commercial only.
- Present a modified build as the official one, or imply I endorse it.

## What counts as the official app
Only builds signed with my key and published on the Releases page of github.com/shanfxr3-cmyk/Jarvis-Voice-Only are official. The About screen tells you if your copy is official. Anything else is someone else's build, and I can't vouch for what's in it.

I have no server and no way to switch anyone off, so this relies on you. If you break these rules, you're not authorised to use the official app, and the licence on the code may be affected as the law allows. Found abuse or a copy that strips credit? Email me.
